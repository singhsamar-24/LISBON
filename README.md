## 🌟 What is LISBON?

**LISBON** is a deeply personal AI life manager — a loyal digital butler built to know *you* completely.

Unlike generic AI assistants, LISBON integrates with every part of your life:
- Reads your **emails** and drafts replies in your tone
- Monitors your **chats** and keeps you updated on what matters
- Manages your **calendar** and plans your day intelligently
- Tracks your **life goals** and keeps you accountable
- Knows your **current situations** and provides context-aware help
- Searches the **internet** for optimal answers — but works offline too

LISBON is not a tool you use. It's a presence that runs alongside your life.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Loyalty** | Built for one person — you. Not a generic product. |
| **Context** | Always aware of your full life situation |
| **Offline-first** | Works without internet; enhanced when connected |
| **Privacy** | Your data stays on your machine |
| **Proactive** | Doesn't wait to be asked — acts when needed |
